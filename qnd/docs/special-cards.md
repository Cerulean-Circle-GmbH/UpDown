# UpDown — Special Cards

## Overview

Each player receives a starter inventory of special cards at game start. Special cards add strategic depth — use them to protect yourself, sabotage opponents, or gain information.

## Starter Inventory

Every player starts with:
- 🛡️ **Protective Shell** (guaranteed)
- 1 random **Level 1** card
- 1 random **Level 2** card

Total: 3 cards per player at game start.

## Using Special Cards

- Play a special card **during a round** (before the result is revealed)
- Each card can only be used **once per game**
- Some cards require selecting a **target player**
- Cards resolve **after all guesses** but **before results are applied**

## Resolution Priority

When multiple special cards are played in the same round, they resolve in priority order:

**Level 3 first → Level 2 → Level 1**

This means a Level 3 card's effect happens before a Level 1 card, which can change outcomes. For example, a Level 3 "One for the Team" resolves before a Level 1 "Protective Shell" — if the team save triggers, the shell wasn't needed.

Identical cards don't stack — if two players play the same card, only the first takes effect.

## Level 1 — Self-Only, Low Impact

### 🛡️ Protective Shell
**Target:** Self
**Effect:** Survive this round no matter what. Even if your guess is wrong, you stay alive.
**Strategy:** Save for a round where you're unsure. Guaranteed in every starter inventory.

### 🧠 Mass Intelligence
**Target:** Self
**Effect:** Survive if more than 50% of all players guessed correctly this round.
**Strategy:** Bet on the crowd being right. Useless in small games where one wrong guess = majority wrong.

### 💰 Double Points
**Target:** Self
**Effect:** If your guess is correct, your round score is doubled.
**Strategy:** Use when you're confident in your guess and have a high streak (doubling 10+5 streak = 30 pts).

### 👁️ Peek
**Target:** Self
**Effect:** See one random card from the GM's hand. The peeked card is revealed in your round results.
**Strategy:** Information advantage for future rounds. Doesn't directly help this round's guess.

---

## Level 2 — Can Target Others, One Round

### 💀 Sacrifice
**Target:** Another player
**Effect:** Eliminate the target player this round, even if they guessed correctly.
**Strategy:** Remove a high-scoring opponent. Ruthless but effective. Can be countered by Protective Shell.

### 🔄 Swap
**Target:** Another player
**Effect:** Swap your guess result (correct/wrong and alive/eliminated) with the target.
**Strategy:** If you're sure you're wrong and they're right — swap to steal their survival.

### 🃏 Reveal Hand
**Target:** All players
**Effect:** The GM's entire hand is revealed to all players this round.
**Strategy:** Helps everyone equally. Play it when information benefits you more than others.

### 🧊 Freeze
**Target:** Another player
**Effect:** The target cannot play a card next round. Their guess is skipped (and they survive — frozen, not eliminated).
**Strategy:** Slow down a player on a hot streak. They lose their streak bonus but stay alive.

---

## Level 3 — High Impact, Multi-Player

### 🤝 One for the Team
**Target:** All players
**Effect:** If you are eliminated this round, ALL other players survive regardless of their guess.
**Strategy:** Heroic sacrifice. Play when you expect to die — you save everyone else. Doesn't help if you survive.

### 🔮 Second Chance
**Target:** Self
**Effect:** If eliminated this round, return to the game next round. One-time revival.
**Strategy:** Insurance policy. Play when stakes are high and you want a safety net.

### 🏴‍☠️ Point Steal
**Target:** Another player
**Effect:** Steal 20 points from the target player (or their remaining points if less than 20).
**Strategy:** Direct score theft. Best used against the leading player late in the game.
